Geopolitical Risk

Strategic Vulnerabilities

Mapping the concentration risks, export controls, and decoupling strategies reshaping the global semiconductor landscape.

CRITICAL

Taiwan Strait Concentration

87% of sub-5nm logic is fabricated by TSMC in Taiwan. A military conflict or blockade would erase $1–2 trillion from global GDP within the first year.

87% concentration
HIGH

ASML EUV Monopoly

100% of EUV lithography systems come from a single Dutch company. Lead times exceed 18–24 months with annual output capped at ~50–60 units.

100% monopoly
HIGH

US Export Controls

BIS entity list bans block EUV tools and advanced EDA to China. HiSilicon forced onto SMIC 7nm DUV multi-patterning at >$140/die costs.

75% supply disruption
MEDIUM

ARM Licensing Disputes

99%+ of mobile SoCs rely on ARM ISA. Qualcomm vs. ARM litigation threatens architectural licensing stability for the entire ecosystem.

55% ecosystem exposure
